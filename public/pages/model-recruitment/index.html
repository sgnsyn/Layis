About LAYIS

LAYIS is a captivating luxury fashion brand that reimagines cultural heritage through a modern lens, blending exquisite craftsmanship with storytelling that resonates across generations. Rooted in the rich tapestry of traditional attire, think flowing robes, intricately embroidered shawls, and symbolic accessories

LAYIS luxury unique identity is designed for exceptional looks of any age (especially for The Eternal Bond collection where mother-child storytelling is key).

Expected Values

Demonstrate discipline, effective time management, and respect in all interactions.

Go beyond wearing garments by conveying the emotional depth, legacy, and intimacy of LAYIS, particularly for The Eternal Bond collection, which emphasizes mother-child narratives.

Maintain openness to working closely with the creative director, artists, and photographers to bring the brand’s vision to life.
